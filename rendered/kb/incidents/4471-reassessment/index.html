The queue did not begin when the desk merged it

The two series measure different events. Received dates describe incoming reports; merge dates describe filing. A large merge batch cannot date the beginning of the incident. Continued reports also make “known” a workflow label, not evidence of resolution.

Why the old dedupe rule concealed the hint defect

The triage prose and accepted decision 0001 say to merge by normalized subject. Their old review date triggers the layout’s stale warning. More importantly, the live component below computes cause equality, not subject equality. The written rule and running behavior disagree.

The examples show both failure directions: the same subject can describe different causes, and different subjects can describe the same cause. With no tenant on file, the component declines to establish a common cause.

Decision 0002 replaces the subject-only rule for this reassessment. Decision 0001 remains intact as an accepted historical record. Use subject matches only to find candidates, then check tenant locale, booking form, channel and symptom evidence. Merge only reports with an established common cause; keep unresolved evidence visible.
