0002 · Establish a common cause before merging

owner lp with omp proposed reviewed 2026-10-28 · 0 days ago

Context

Decision 0001 merged reports solely by normalized subject. The reassessment of #4471 finds that the incident’s own computed cause table separates a shared-parser defect from a booking-v3 hint defect. The live triage explorer also compares causes rather than subjects. The old accepted record is preserved; this proposal replaces its operational rule for the reassessment, pending desk acceptance.

Decision

Use matching subjects to find candidates, not as evidence of a common problem. Look up the tenant’s locale and booking-form version and inspect the entry channel and symptom. For the exported date classifier, merge only when sameCause establishes a non-null common cause. Keep parser and hint reports in separate issues even when their subjects match. Different subjects do not justify splitting reports when their cause is established to be the same.

Unknown tenants and contradictory symptoms need investigation, not automatic merging. In particular, retain the timing anomalies as unresolved evidence rather than claiming that their filing category proves their root cause. Deduplicate export overlap by report ID before counting.
